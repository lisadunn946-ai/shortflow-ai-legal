ShortFlow AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ShortFlow AI processes information when you use its video analysis, optimization, account connection, publishing, and scheduling features.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, ShortFlow AI may process information such as account identifiers, display names, profile information, authorization information, uploaded videos, video metadata, generated captions, titles, descriptions, tags, publishing settings, and publication status.

2. Connected Social Media Accounts

When you connect a supported social media account, ShortFlow AI uses the platform's official authorization process. Access is limited to permissions authorized by you and required for the requested features.

3. TikTok Integration

When you connect a TikTok account, ShortFlow AI may receive information permitted by the scopes you authorize, such as basic profile information and permissions necessary to upload or publish content.

ShortFlow AI uses TikTok information only to provide features requested by the user, including account identification and authorized content publishing functionality.

4. Video and Content Processing

Videos and associated metadata may be processed to provide features such as content analysis, transcription, metadata generation, optimization, scheduling, and publishing.

5. Artificial Intelligence Processing

ShortFlow AI may use AI services to analyze submitted content and generate suggestions such as titles, descriptions, captions, tags, keywords, or other publishing metadata.

6. How Information Is Used

Information is processed for purposes including:

  • Providing and operating ShortFlow AI features.
  • Connecting authorized social media accounts.
  • Analyzing and optimizing short-form video content.
  • Uploading, publishing, or scheduling content requested by users.
  • Maintaining publishing status and preventing duplicate operations.
  • Improving reliability and security of the service.

7. Sharing of Information

ShortFlow AI does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to third-party platforms or service providers only when necessary to perform requested functionality, such as AI processing or publishing content to an authorized social media account.

8. Data Security

ShortFlow AI uses reasonable technical safeguards designed to protect stored credentials and authorization information. Sensitive integration credentials should not be publicly exposed or intentionally shared.

9. Data Retention

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, maintain application state, comply with applicable requirements, and support authorized account integrations.

10. Account Disconnection and Data Deletion

Users may disconnect supported social media accounts from ShortFlow AI. Where applicable, users may also revoke ShortFlow AI's authorization through the connected platform's account or security settings.

Requests concerning deletion of information associated with ShortFlow AI may be submitted through the official ShortFlow AI contact channel.

11. Third-Party Services

Third-party services integrated with ShortFlow AI operate under their own privacy policies and terms. ShortFlow AI does not control the privacy practices of those independent services.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ShortFlow AI's features, integrations, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

13. Contact

Questions or privacy-related requests may be submitted through the official ShortFlow AI contact channel made available by the service.
